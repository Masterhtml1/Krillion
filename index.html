    How to play

    1. Every day there's a new Daily Dive of 7 prompts, the same for everyone.
    2. You get 25 seconds of air per prompt. Type one answer that fits it exactly. If it isn't on the list, keep trying while the clock runs.
    3. Accepted answers land in one of six rarity tiers. Common answers drift toward Plankton; answers few people reach drift toward One in a Krillion.
    4. Every point sinks you 10 metres. A perfect dive is 700 points: 7,000 m, down in the trench.

    Rules in brackets decide what counts. Rarity never bends the wording: an answer has to fit the prompt.

    More ways to dive

    • Unlimited Dive: a fresh random set of 7 prompts, as often as you like.
    • Endless Dive: no prompt limit. A miss costs one of your 3 air tanks; a Deep Cut or One in a Krillion refills one.
    • Themed Packs: single-topic dives (movies, food, sport and more).
    • Archive: replay any past daily dive.
